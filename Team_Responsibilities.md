# 👥 College Cultural Fest – Team Responsibilities

## 1. Organizing Team
- Plan and coordinate the entire event.
- Manage the event schedule.
- Coordinate with college management.

## 2. Finance Team
- Prepare the event budget.
- Track all expenses.
- Maintain financial records.

## 3. Technical Team
- Arrange microphones, speakers and projectors.
- Manage technical requirements.
- Handle audio and visual support.

## 4. Decoration Team
- Decorate the auditorium and stage.
- Arrange banners and welcome boards.

## 5. Food Team
- Arrange food and refreshments.
- Coordinate food distribution.

## 6. Media Team
- Capture photos and videos.
- Prepare event announcements.
- Share event updates.

## 7. Volunteer Team
- Guide participants and guests.
- Manage registrations.
- Support all event activities.
